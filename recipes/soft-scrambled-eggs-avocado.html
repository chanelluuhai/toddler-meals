Soft scrambled eggs + avocado toast fingers

~10 minutes · soft textures · 1 toddler serving

Home Sample week

Soft scrambled eggs with mashed avocado on toast strips. Done in about 10 minutes.

Ingredients / grocery

  • 1–2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon butter or oil
  • 1 slice bread
  • 1/2 ripe avocado

Instructions

  1. Soft-scramble the eggs in butter over low heat until just set but still slightly wet.
  2. Toast the bread lightly. Mash avocado on top and cut into finger strips.
  3. Cool eggs and toast to warm (not hot). Serve eggs in a small bowl beside the toast fingers.
