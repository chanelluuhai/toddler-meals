Mini turkey meatball bowls

~25 minutes · soft bowl · variety beyond pasta

Home Sample week

Marble-size turkey meatballs over soft rice with peas — finger-friendly and not another pasta night.

Ingredients / grocery

  • 1/2 pound ground turkey
  • 1/4 cup breadcrumbs or crushed crackers
  • 1 egg
  • Pinch of salt
  • Cooked soft rice or quinoa
  • 1 handful frozen peas

Instructions

  1. Mix ground turkey with breadcrumbs, egg, and a pinch of salt.
  2. Roll marble-size balls and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 12–15 minutes until cooked through.
  3. Warm peas until soft. Serve 3–4 meatballs over soft rice with peas. Cool to warm before serving.
