Cheese quesadilla wedges

~8 minutes · finger food · 1 toddler serving

Home Sample week

Soft cheese quesadilla cut into thin wedges. Keep the heat low so edges stay soft.

Ingredients / grocery

  • 1 small flour tortilla
  • 1/4 cup shredded mild cheese
  • Optional: 1 spoon mashed black beans
  • Optional: mashed avocado for serving

Instructions

  1. Warm the tortilla in a dry pan on medium-low heat.
  2. Sprinkle cheese on half (add mashed beans if using), then fold.
  3. Cook until cheese melts and both sides are lightly golden, about 2 minutes per side. Keep heat low.
  4. Cool, then cut into thin wedges. Serve with avocado on the side if you like.
